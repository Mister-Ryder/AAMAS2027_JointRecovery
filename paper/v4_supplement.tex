\section{Feature and Execution Details}
\paragraph{Observable inputs.}
The reward scale is $s_G=\max\{1,\sum_i w_i/|V|\}$.
Each recovery vertex supplies normalized reward, log degree, local degree
fraction, actual warm membership, log factor memberships and log maximum
incident factor size. The structural encoder has two layers of width 32.
Its shared mean and reward-weighted pooled encodings each have width 32.
The seven request features are the seconds/nodes/iterations indicators,
log native allowance, log remaining seconds, normalized actual warm value
and actual warm fraction. The thirteen summary features are total,
maximum, mean and standard deviation of normalized recovery reward;
log edge count and density; log factor count, maximum/mean factor size,
mean memberships and high-order factor fraction; clique-load priority P1;
and the clique-partition upper value. The immediate gain is an exact offset,
not a learned input. The evaluated version uses these features directly;
it does not contain the subsequent untrained normalization proposal.

\paragraph{Cache boundary.}
A structural unit is identified by its graph/model version, ordered recovery
vertices, factors, actual warm membership and reward scale. Different native
allowances share a unit. Changed warm membership creates a new unit.
No target membership, execution score or previous graph enters the cache.
Offline training uses attached encodings; online encodings are detached.
Each decision starts with empty caches. Packing, bound calculation, device
transfer, cache lookup and inference are charged to the decision.

\paragraph{Fractional capacity property.}
Let $s_F=\sum_{j\in F}p_j$ and define
\[
d_i=\max\{1,\max_{F\ni i}s_F\}.
\]
For each factor $F$ with $s_F>0$,
$d_i\geq s_F$ for every $i\in F$, giving
$\sum_{i\in F}p_i/d_i\leq s_F/s_F=1$. When $s_F=0$ the sum is zero.
Thus simultaneous contraction satisfies the covered clique inequalities.
It does not establish integral feasibility, and the fractional weighted sum
is only an input to the bounded scalar head. Feasible memberships are
produced and checked by the executor.

\paragraph{Bounds and empty cases.}
The warm lower value is from an actually executed feasible membership.
The upper value sums the maximum reward in each disjoint clique block plus
leftover singleton rewards. Hence every independent recovery lies below it.
Empty recovery scopes have zero recovery; empty loss means are zero.
Floating summation noise may raise an upper value to its warm lower value
within the declared absolute/relative tolerance, but cannot replace original
integer objectives or membership checks.

\section{Benchmark Roles and Cost Boundaries}
The main controlled comparison uses all 24 validation graphs, eleven policies
and three total deadlines. Each learned policy has three fitting seeds,
giving 1,512 runs. Within each graph, fit means precede equal menu/resource
aggregation. Zero opportunities, failures and late returns retain their weight.
The fixed common greedy prefix is included in total returned quality and
subtracted separately for additional recovery. A result received after its
deadline has zero strict gain regardless of an internally available candidate.

The public profile retains all 40 original unit-weight graphs: 16 DIMACS clique
complements, 18 SATLIB UF and six CBS conflict graphs. Seven native
configurations at two soft workpoints give 1,680 runs; three released neural
recipes give 360, for 2,040 runs total. Family means have equal mass after
three initial schedules are averaged within each graph. Complete native cold
cost includes export, startup, preprocessing, parsing and validation. Resident
neural request cost excludes once-only setup. DIFUSCO one/four-sample
parent/worker setup is 5.948/5.785 and 5.946/5.828 seconds; COExpander setup
is 7.238/7.125 seconds. These boundaries preclude a common-budget speedup claim.

Whole-graph profiles compare published solvers and establish context for the
shared repair kernel. They do not test our learned policy on the same public
graphs. Released checkpoint overlap remains unresolved. The current controlled
development results also do not establish independent confirmation or full
application-scale transfer. The companion data tables retain these roles
explicitly; future experimental designs are not presented as completed evidence.
