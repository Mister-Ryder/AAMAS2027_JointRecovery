# Scientific companion for the current frozen version

Includes manuscript sources, deterministic vector/curve sources, original 96-graph labels, all 15 fit histories/checkpoints, and complete aggregate/graph-level result tables.
The evaluated model is the original residual model; no untrained normalization candidate is included.
Controlled comparison: 1512 runs; public published-solver profiles: 2040 runs.
The public profiles do not establish transfer superiority of the learned policy.
Full original online/native output archives remain in the local research workspace; this compact companion does not duplicate every process log or external executable.
Published algorithms and released neural checkpoints must be obtained from their official releases under their own licenses.
Actual run-argument JSONs retain original execution paths: adapt paths when replaying, preserving scientific bindings.
Actual timing was measured with Linux, Python 3.8, Torch 1.11/CUDA 11.3 and an RTX 4080. Different hardware does not reproduce exact caller timing.
Research assistance and incomplete historical prompt identifiers are disclosed in the companion PDF.
